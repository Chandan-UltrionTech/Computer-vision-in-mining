# PART III — TURNING ORE INTO VALUE & SEEING THE WHOLE MINE  
### Sorting → Processing → Recovery → Stockpile → Survey → Inspection → Whole-Mine Reveal

Part III should feel different again.

Part I was:

> **Understand the rock.**

Part II was:

> **Move the rock.**

Part III becomes:

> **Decide what has value → recover it → understand the entire operation from above.**

Visually, we move from very close-up individual particles to an enormous final view of the complete mine.

So the camera scale itself tells the story:

**particle → process → plant → stockpile → landscape → entire mine.**

That gives the ending a natural sense of expansion.
